Session ID and recovery

The session ID is the login for your WhatsApp account. Anyone who has it controls the account, so never post it publicly.

If the status says Logged out or Restricted: a logout (401) is not always a ban, it also happens when a link fails. If the number really is banned, no panel or bot can lift it. Open WhatsApp on that phone, tap "Request a review" on the ban screen and wait for their reply. Meanwhile link a different number here and your settings stay. Keep a copy of your session ID: if your server changes, paste it above to reconnect without a new code. It only works while the account is still active.
